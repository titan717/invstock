How to play

Buy shares when you think a company is undervalued, then sell when the price rises. Prices move once per simulated minute. News can create sudden opportunities or losses. Savings earn interest once per simulated day. Loans add leverage but carry interest and can trigger forced selling if your net worth falls below the debt. Buildings generate passive income. All money is virtual and your game is saved only on this device.
